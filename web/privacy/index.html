GlucOS privacy

Updated 11 November 2026

GlucOS is a personal project, not a company. It shows glucose readings from a FreeStyle Libre sensor through Abbott's LibreLinkUp service. It has no ads and no analytics, and it never sells or shares your data with anyone for marketing.

If you wear a sensor

  • Your LibreLinkUp login stays in your iPhone's Keychain. GlucOS uses it to fetch your readings from LibreLinkUp.
  • Readings are kept on your devices for up to 90 days so charts and time-in-range work.
  • Logbook sync. Insulin, food, site changes and events you log are stored in the GlucOS database (Supabase, in the cloud) so they sync between your Mac and iPhone. Your login is checked with Abbott once to set this up and isn't kept. Each person can only reach their own entries.
  • Push service. Only on the developer's own iPhone, a server checks LibreLinkUp every minute and sends alerts and Lock Screen updates through Apple Push Notifications. It keeps that one account's login encrypted and up to 90 days of its readings. Other people's logins are never sent to it.

Sharing and following

  • A share link lets anyone who has it see the sharer's current reading, trend and last 3 hours. The database keeps only a fingerprint (hash) of the link, not the link itself. Links can expire, and the sharer can stop one at any time.
  • If you follow someone, your iPhone sends the share service a random install ID and Apple push tokens, so their reading can stay on your Lock Screen and their alerts can reach you. You don't need an account, and no name, email or phone number is collected. Stopping following deletes this.
  • Opening a link in a browser sends the link only to the GlucOS share service, never to the web server that hosts the page.

Apple and TestFlight

If you test GlucOS through TestFlight, Apple shares with the developer what TestFlight always does: your name or email as you joined, crash reports and any feedback you send. See Apple's TestFlight terms for details.

Removing your data

  • Sign out in Settings, or delete the app, to remove your login and readings from your phone.
  • Stop sharing a link, or stop following someone, to delete its records straight away.
  • To have your synced logbook deleted, open an issue on GitHub or reply to the TestFlight feedback. It's deleted within 30 days.

Not a medical device

GlucOS is not a medical device and doesn't give treatment advice. Readings and alerts can be delayed or missed. Always confirm with your sensor app or meter before acting.
